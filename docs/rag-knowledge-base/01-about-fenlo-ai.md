# About Fenlo AI

## What Is Fenlo AI?

Fenlo AI is an AI automation services company that builds custom AI-powered chatbots, voice agents, and multi-channel communication systems for businesses. We design, build, and deploy production-grade AI solutions tailored to each client's specific needs — not templates, not no-code builders, but fully custom-engineered systems.

Our platform is live at **bot.fenloai.com**, where clients can explore interactive demos of all three solutions, view analytics dashboards, and manage their AI bots.

## Who Built It?

Fenlo AI was built by **Mohammad Shoaib**, a full-stack AI engineer specializing in conversational AI, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), voice AI, and multi-channel deployment. Mohammad combines deep backend engineering expertise (Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL) with modern frontend development (Next.js, React, TypeScript) and applied AI/ML (OpenAI, Groq/Llama, Pinecone, vector embeddings).

## What Does Fenlo AI Do?

Fenlo AI helps businesses automate customer interactions across multiple channels. We offer three core solutions:

1. **RAGChat** — Document-powered AI chatbots that answer questions using your own knowledge base, with source citations and zero hallucination guesswork.
2. **VoiceBot Pro** — AI phone agents that handle calls with natural voice conversations, real-time transcription, and intelligent escalation to human agents.
3. **OmniBot** — Multi-channel AI deployment across WhatsApp, website chat widgets, Telegram, and webhook integrations, all managed from a unified inbox.

All three solutions are live and fully functional on the platform. They share a common enterprise-grade backend with analytics, authentication, role-based access control, and workspace isolation.
