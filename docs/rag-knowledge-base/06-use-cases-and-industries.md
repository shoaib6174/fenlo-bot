# Use Cases and Industries

Fenlo AI solutions are versatile and can be adapted to virtually any industry where customer communication needs to be automated, enhanced, or scaled. Below are industry-specific use cases with examples of the kinds of questions the AI chatbot can handle.

---

## E-Commerce

Online stores can use Fenlo AI to automate customer support, answer product questions, track orders, and capture leads.

### Applicable Solutions
- **RAGChat** — Product FAQ bot trained on product catalogs, return policies, and shipping information.
- **OmniBot** — Deploy on website chat widget and WhatsApp for omnichannel support.
- **VoiceBot Pro** — Handle order status calls and post-purchase support.

### Example Questions the Bot Can Handle
- "What is your return policy?"
- "How long does shipping take to Dhaka?"
- "Is this product available in size XL?"
- "Where is my order? My tracking number is BF12345."
- "Do you offer cash on delivery?"
- "Can I exchange this item for a different color?"
- "What payment methods do you accept?"
- "Are there any ongoing discounts or promotions?"

### Key Features Used
- Knowledge base loaded with product catalogs, shipping policies, and FAQ documents
- WhatsApp integration for order updates and customer inquiries
- Website chat widget on product pages for instant answers
- Lead scoring to identify high-intent shoppers
- Sentiment analysis to flag frustrated customers for human follow-up

## Cross-Industry Features

Regardless of your industry, every Fenlo AI deployment includes:

- **Analytics Dashboard**: Track conversation volume, sentiment trends, popular topics, and response quality across all channels.
- **Lead Scoring**: Automatically identify high-value prospects based on conversation signals like pricing inquiries, timeline mentions, and purchase intent.
- **Knowledge Gap Detection**: Continuously identify what your knowledge base is missing and improve your AI's accuracy over time.
- **Human Handoff**: Seamlessly transfer conversations to human agents when the AI encounters questions it cannot answer or when the situation requires a personal touch.
- **Multi-Channel Deployment**: Reach your customers wherever they are — website, WhatsApp, Telegram, phone, or webhook integrations.
- **Source Citations**: Every AI response references the source document, building trust and enabling verification.
- **Continuous Improvement**: Analytics and gap detection create a feedback loop that makes your AI smarter with every conversation.
