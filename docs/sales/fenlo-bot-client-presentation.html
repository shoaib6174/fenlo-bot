The Core Idea

An AI chatbot trained on your company's own documents that answers questions accurately

You give us your files — product catalogs, HR policies, pricing sheets, SOPs, manuals — and we build an intelligent chatbot. Anyone can talk to it on WhatsApp, your website, or an internal link. In Bangla and English.

Think of it like giving every person — customer, employee, or field sales rep — instant access to a colleague who has read every document the company has ever created. Available 24/7.

Frequently Asked

Questions You'll Have

"Is our data safe?"

Every client's data is completely isolated. No one can see another client's information. All connections encrypted. Export or delete anytime.

"What if the bot gives wrong answers?"

The bot only answers from your documents — never makes things up. Every answer includes a source reference. If unsure, it says so or transfers to a human.

"How much does it cost?"

No fixed packages. We build custom and price based on actual scope — channels, documents, complexity. You pay for what you need.

"Can we try it first?"

Yes. We set up a demo using your own documents so you see exactly how it works for your business.

"Can it book appointments?"

Yes. Collects all details in conversation. We integrate with your existing booking system — or build a simple one for you.

"Do we need a technical person?"

No. Dashboard is designed for non-technical people. Uploading documents is like attaching a file to an email. We handle all technical setup.

"Will this replace our employees?"

No. The bot handles repetitive tasks — same questions, looking up info, collecting details. It frees your team for work that needs human judgment.

"What if internet/power goes down?"

Bot runs on cloud servers. During load-shedding, anyone with mobile data can still reach the bot. It never stops.
