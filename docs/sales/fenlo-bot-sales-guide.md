## 1. What Is Fenlo Bot?

Fenlo Bot is one thing at its core: **an AI chatbot trained on your company's own documents that answers questions accurately — in Bangla and English.**

You give us your files — product catalogs, HR policies, pricing sheets, SOPs, manuals, FAQs — and we build an intelligent chatbot that anyone can talk to on WhatsApp, your website, or an internal link. The bot reads everything, understands questions naturally, and gives answers with proof (showing exactly which document and section the answer came from).

Think of it like giving every person — customer, employee, or field sales rep — instant access to a colleague who has read every single document the company has ever created. And that colleague is available 24/7, never forgets anything, never gives inconsistent answers, and works during Eid, hartals, and load-shedding.

**What we sell is not a fixed product.** We build custom chatbot solutions for each client based on what their business actually needs. Every client gets a system tailored to their documents, their workflow, and their people. We price based on scope, not fixed packages.

**Three key things to remember:**
1. It's a **chatbot** — text-based conversations, not phone calls (we have a voice add-on for English, but our core strength is chat)
2. It works in **Bangla and English** — even mixed, which is how most Bangladeshis communicate
3. It's **not just for customers** — companies get the biggest value using it for their own teams (HR, sales, training, operations)

## 2. How It Works

The bot works the same way regardless of who uses it. Here's the simple version:

### Step 1: Upload Documents
The client gives us their files — PDFs, Word documents, text files, spreadsheets. These could be:
- Product catalogs and price lists
- Company policies and HR handbooks
- SOPs and compliance manuals
- FAQs and training materials
- Sales playbooks and competitor comparisons
- Any written knowledge the business has

### Step 2: The AI Reads and Learns
Our system breaks down every document, understands the content, and makes it searchable. This happens automatically — no manual work from the client.

### Step 3: People Ask Questions
Through WhatsApp, website chat, or an internal link — anyone authorized can ask the bot a question in natural Bangla or English. Just like messaging a person.

### Step 4: The Bot Answers With Proof
The bot finds the relevant information from the documents and gives an accurate answer. Every answer includes a reference to which document and which section it came from — so the answer can be verified.

### Step 5: The Bot Gets Smarter Over Time
If someone asks a question the documents don't cover, the system logs it as a "knowledge gap." The client can review these gaps and upload new content to fill them. The bot keeps improving.

### What Happens When the Bot Can't Answer?
- It honestly says it doesn't have that information — it doesn't make things up
- It can hand over the conversation to a human team member seamlessly
- It logs the unanswered question so the client knows what to add

### Can It Do More Than Answer Questions?
Yes. The bot can also:
- **Book appointments** — collect date, time, service type, and contact details from the customer. We either integrate with the client's existing booking system or build a simple appointment management system for them
- **Collect leads** — capture customer details, budget, preferences, and flag hot leads in the dashboard
- **Take structured orders** — collect items, quantity, delivery address, and preferred time (especially useful on WhatsApp)
- **Route conversations** — hand off to the right team member based on topic, urgency, or customer mood

## 3. Three Ways Businesses Use Fenlo Bot

This is the most important section to understand. We're not selling three separate products — we're selling one chatbot that serves **three different audiences**. A client might use one, two, or all three.

### A. For Customers — "Your Business, Available 24/7 on WhatsApp"

The bot sits on the company's WhatsApp and/or website. Customers message it and get instant, accurate answers from the company's own documents.

**What the bot does for customers:**
- Answers product questions ("Is this available in XL?", "What's the delivery charge to Chittagong?")
- Provides pricing and service information
- Books appointments and collects order details
- Answers FAQs (hours, location, return policy, warranty)
- Captures lead information (name, contact, what they're interested in)
- Hands over to a human when the question is too complex

**Why this matters for the client:**
- Customers get instant replies — even at 2 AM, on Fridays, during Eid
- One bot handles hundreds of conversations simultaneously (no hiring extra staff)
- Every conversation is tracked in a dashboard — no lead falls through the cracks
- The team only handles conversations that actually need a human
- Works in Bangla — most Bangladeshi customers prefer messaging in Bangla or mixed Bangla-English

**Example (E-Commerce):**
A fashion brand gets 200+ WhatsApp messages a day asking the same questions: "Is this in stock?", "Delivery charge to Rajshahi?", "Cash on delivery available?" Right now, one person types every reply manually. With Fenlo Bot, the bot handles 70-80% of these automatically from the product catalog. **Example (Diagnostic Center):**
Patients message on WhatsApp asking "What time does Dr. Rahman sit?", "How much is a CBC test?", "When can I collect my report?" The bot answers instantly from the hospital's schedule and price list — in Bangla. Patients can even book appointments through the bot. The reception team handles only complex cases and emergencies.

## 4. How It Reaches People (Channels)

The chatbot is one brain that can talk to people through different channels. Here's what we offer:

### WhatsApp (Primary — Most Important for Bangladesh)

WhatsApp is how Bangladesh communicates. It's our most important channel.

- Connected via the client's WhatsApp Business account
- Customers, employees, or sales reps message the business number and the bot responds
- Full Bangla support
- Works on basic 2G data (just text messages)
- No app to install — everyone already has WhatsApp
- Bot can send text, links, and structured information

### Website Chat Widget

A small chat bubble that appears on the client's website.

- Visitors click it and a chat window opens
- The bot greets them and starts answering questions
- We customize colors and branding to match the website
- Lightweight — doesn't slow down the website
- Works perfectly on mobile phones (important — most BD users browse on mobile)
- Visitors don't need to give their phone number — lower barrier than WhatsApp

### Unified Inbox

All conversations from all channels show up in one dashboard.

- WhatsApp messages, website chats — all in one place
- The team sees every conversation, sorted by status, channel, and lead score
- Any team member can jump into a conversation when the bot needs help (human handoff)
- The customer doesn't notice when a human takes over — it's seamless

### Phone Agent (Add-On — English Only)

We also offer an AI phone agent that can answer voice calls.

**Important for the sales team:** Phone Agent currently works in **English only**. Bangla voice is not yet available. Only offer this when:
- The client deals with English-speaking callers (international businesses, buying houses, NGOs, corporate B2B)
- The client wants basic call routing (answer → collect info → transfer to the right person)

For Bangla-speaking customers, always recommend WhatsApp chat instead — it supports Bangla perfectly and is where most Bangladeshis prefer to communicate anyway.

**What Phone Agent can do:**
- Answer calls 24/7 with natural-sounding English conversation
- Transfer calls based on rules (keywords, caller frustration, confidence level, time of day)
- Record and transcribe every call
- Track leads from callers

## 9. Common Questions Clients Will Ask (FAQ)

### "Is it safe? Will it leak our data?"

Every client's data is completely separate from every other client. No one can see another client's information. All connections are encrypted. We provide data export and deletion tools so clients stay in full control.

### "What if the bot gives a wrong answer?"

The bot only answers based on the documents we train it with. It doesn't make things up. Every answer includes a reference to the source document, so answers can be verified. If the bot isn't confident enough, it says so or transfers to a human — it doesn't guess.

The system also tracks questions it couldn't answer. The client reviews these and adds the missing information, making the bot smarter over time.

### "Can the bot understand Bangla?"

Yes — and this is one of our biggest strengths. The chatbot fully understands and responds in Bangla, English, or a mix of both — which is how most Bangladeshis naturally communicate. Upload Bangla documents and the bot answers in Bangla. Upload English and it answers in English. Mixed works too.

**Note**: Our Phone Agent add-on (voice calls) currently works in English only. For Bangla-speaking users, we always recommend WhatsApp and website chat — which handle Bangla perfectly.

### "Can the bot book appointments?"

Yes. The bot can collect all appointment details — date, time, service type, customer name and contact — directly in the conversation. If the client already has a booking or scheduling system, we integrate the bot with it. If they don't have one, we can build a simple appointment management system for them as part of the solution.

### "Can the bot take orders?"

Yes. Especially useful on WhatsApp — the bot collects order details in a structured way (items, quantity, delivery address, preferred time). This information goes into the dashboard for the team, or we integrate it with the client's existing order management system.

### "How long does it take to set up?"

It depends on scope and how ready the client's materials are. If they have their documents organized, basic setups are fast. If they need to create documentation first or have complex workflows with multiple departments, it takes longer. We assess each project individually.

### "What if the internet goes down at our office?"

The bot runs on cloud servers, not on the client's computer or office network. So even during load-shedding or office internet issues, the bot keeps working for anyone who can reach it. A customer or employee with mobile data can still chat with the bot.

### "Can we change what the bot says?"

Absolutely. The client can update documents, change the bot's personality and tone, modify settings anytime through the dashboard. Upload new documents, remove outdated ones — no technical skills needed.

### "Do we need a technical person to manage this?"

No. The dashboard is designed for non-technical people. Uploading documents is as simple as attaching a file to an email. Changing settings is clicking buttons and filling forms. We handle all technical setup and maintenance.

### "Can we try it before committing?"

Yes. We can set up a demo using the client's own documents so they see exactly how it works for their business.

### "How much does it cost?"

We don't have fixed packages. Every business is different, so we build a custom solution and price based on actual scope — what the bot needs to do, how many channels, how many documents, how complex the setup. The client pays for what they need, not for features they'll never use.

### "We already have a Facebook page / WhatsApp Business. Why do we need this?"

Having WhatsApp is like having a phone number — people can reach you, but someone still has to read every message and type every reply. Fenlo Bot reads and responds automatically, 24/7, from your own documents. Your team only handles conversations that actually need a human.

### "Can our competitors see our data?"

No. Every client's system is completely isolated. Documents, conversations, and data are invisible to everyone else.

### "Will this replace our employees?"

No, and we never position it that way. The bot handles repetitive tasks — answering the same questions, looking up information from documents, collecting details from customers. This frees employees to do work that actually requires human judgment, creativity, and personal touch. Think of it as a tool that makes the team more productive, not a replacement.

### "How is this different from a basic chatbot?"

Basic chatbots follow a fixed script — "If customer says X, reply Y." Our bot actually *understands* questions and gives intelligent answers from the company's own documents. It can handle questions it has never seen before, as long as the answer exists in the documents. It's the difference between a phone menu and a knowledgeable assistant.
