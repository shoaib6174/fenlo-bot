RETURNS & EXCHANGES
-------------------

Return Policy:
- Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
- Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have original tags attached.
- Sale items are eligible for exchange only, not refund.
- Swimwear and intimates are final sale for hygiene reasons.

How to Return:
1. Log into your account at bellasboutique.com/returns
2. Select the order and items you wish to return
3. Print the prepaid shipping label (free for US orders)
4. Drop off at any USPS location
5. Refund processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the item

Exchange Process:
- Follow the same steps as returns
- Select "Exchange" instead of "Return"
- Choose your new size/color
- New item ships as soon as we receive the original

Refund Methods:
- Original payment method: 5-7 business days
- Store credit: Instant, plus 10% bonus credit
- We cannot refund to a different payment method than the original


SHIPPING INFORMATION
--------------------

Domestic Shipping (United States):
- Standard Shipping: Free on orders over $75, otherwise $5.95 (5-7 business days)
- Express Shipping: $12.95 (2-3 business days)
- Overnight Shipping: $24.95 (next business day, order by 2pm EST)

International Shipping (Canada):
- Standard: $9.95 (7-14 business days)
- Express: $19.95 (3-5 business days)
- Duties and taxes are the responsibility of the customer

Order Tracking:
- Tracking number emailed within 24 hours of shipment
- Track at bellasboutique.com/track or via USPS/FedEx
- Text "TRACK" to 1-800-BELLA-55 for SMS updates

Lost or Damaged Packages:
- Report within 48 hours of expected delivery
- We will reship the order at no cost or issue a full refund
- Photo of damaged item required for damage claims
