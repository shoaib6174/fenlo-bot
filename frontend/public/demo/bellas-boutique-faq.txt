BELLA'S BOUTIQUE — Frequently Asked Questions
===============================================

Q: How long does shipping take?
A: Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days within the US. Express shipping
   takes 2-3 business days. Overnight delivery is available for orders placed
   before 2pm EST. Free standard shipping on orders over $75.

Q: What is your return policy?
A: You have 30 days from delivery to return items for a full refund. Items must
   be unworn with original tags. Sale items can be exchanged but not refunded.
   Swimwear and intimates are final sale.

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, we ship to Canada. Standard international shipping is $9.95 (7-14 days)
   and express is $19.95 (3-5 days). Duties and taxes are the customer's
   responsibility.

Q: How do I track my order?
A: You'll receive a tracking number via email within 24 hours of shipment.
   You can also track at bellasboutique.com/track or text "TRACK" to our
   phone number for SMS updates.

Q: What sizes do you carry?
A: We carry XS through XL in most styles. Our sizing runs true to standard
   US sizes. Each product page has a "Find My Size" tool. When in doubt,
   we recommend sizing up.

Q: Do you offer gift cards?
A: Yes! Digital gift cards are available at bellasboutique.com/gift-cards
   in amounts from $25 to $500. They never expire and can be used on any
   purchase including sale items.

Q: What is Bella Rewards?
A: Our loyalty program where you earn 1 point per dollar spent. Bronze members
   get standard benefits, Silver (500+ points) gets 1.5x points and early sale
   access, and Gold (1000+ points) gets 2x points, free express shipping, and
   a birthday gift.

Q: Can I cancel my order?
A: Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After that, the order
   enters fulfillment and can't be cancelled. You can return it after delivery.

Q: Do you price match?
A: Yes, we match prices from major US retailers like Nordstrom, Macy's, and
   ASOS. Request within 14 days of purchase for the same item, size, and color.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: Visa, Mastercard, AmEx, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Afterpay (pay in
   4 interest-free installments). We also accept Bella's Boutique gift cards.

Q: Is your packaging eco-friendly?
A: Yes! All packaging is 100% recyclable and plastic-free. Our Eco Collection
   uses organic cotton and recycled polyester. We've been carbon-neutral on
   all shipments since 2023.

Q: What if I receive a damaged item?
A: Contact us within 48 hours with a photo of the damage. We'll send a
   replacement at no cost or issue a full refund, your choice. No need to
   return the damaged item.
